What is heat index?

Heat index describes how hot it actually feels to your body when air temperature and humidity are considered together.

🌡️

Air temperature

How hot the air is, as a thermometer reads it.

💧

Humidity

How much moisture is in the air. Sweat dries slowly in humid air, so your body cools down less.

🥵

Why it is useful

The thermometer can say 33 °C while your body feels like it is 41 °C. Heat index tells you the feeling that matters for safety.

How does heat affect your body?

Your body cools itself by sweating. When heat and humidity are too high, that system struggles. Scroll down the trail to follow what can happen, from mild to serious.

🚨 Get help right away if you see

Confusion, fainting, a very hot body, hot flushed skin, seizures, or a person who will not wake up. This can be heat stroke, a medical emergency. Move the person to shade, cool them down, and call emergency services or a teacher or medic immediately.

📈 Heat index through the year

In the Philippines the heat index climbs at the end of the dry season, usually around April and May, when hot air and humidity build up together. Rainy months bring it down a little, but humid days can still feel hot.

Sample monthly peak values for a Metro Manila-like location. Dotted lines mark the 27, 33 and 42 °C warning levels.

📊 People lost to heat

Heat is one of the deadliest weather hazards. Older adults, young children, outdoor workers and people with health problems are at the highest risk. Most heat deaths can be prevented with water, shade, rest and checking on others.

Rounded estimates for European summers from published studies. Double-check exact figures before citing them.

🥧 How hot days add up

During the hot season most days sit at Caution or Extreme Caution, but a smaller share reach Danger. Those few days are the ones that call for the strongest care: limit outdoor activity, drink water and rest in cool places.
